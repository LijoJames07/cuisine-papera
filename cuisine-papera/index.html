WELCOME TO CUISINE PAPERA

Gonikoppa's Favorite Destination for Authentic Kodava Feast

Situated at Gowri Complex on the bustling Virajpet–Mysore Road, Cuisine Papera stands as an iconic food haven for travelers, food connoisseurs, and local families seeking genuine Coorg flavors.

Rooted in centuries-old Kodava culinary traditions, our master chefs handcraft dark aromatic Pandi Curry, wood-smoked meats, bamboo-infused rice specialties, and fresh local Coorg produce. Every dish is seasoned with dark Kachampuli (Coorg black vinegar) and slow-cooked in traditional clayware.

Authentic Kodava Cuisine

Time-tested recipes passed down through Coorg ancestral homes.

Traditional Ambience

Warm wooden textures, heritage decor, and relaxed seating.

Family-Friendly Atmosphere

Spacious, clean, and welcoming environment for all groups.

Prime Location

Conveniently located on Virajpet-Mysore Highway with ample parking.
